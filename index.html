Focus areas

What I work on

See full research
  • 01

    LC–MS/MS proteomics & metaproteomics

    Hands-on operation of high-resolution Orbitrap and timsTOF/Bruker instruments. Method development and optimisation for label-free DDA and DIA acquisition across complex biological matrices — tissue, plasma, microbiome and food samples.

  • 02

    Data analysis: MaxQuant, FragPipe, DIA-NN, Spectronaut

    Proficient in MaxQuant and FragPipe for DDA, library-free DIA (DIA-NN, Spectronaut), and timsTOF PASEF/diaPASEF datasets. Experienced with statistical post-processing in Perseus and R/RStudio — differential expression, PTM localisation and quantitative reproducibility.

  • 03

    Peptide chemistry & biophysical analytics

    Solid-phase peptide synthesis, HPLC-MS purification, NMR, CD, ITC and fluorescence assays. Designing biophysical workflows from molecule to mechanism — antimicrobial peptides, foldamers and therapeutic peptides.

  • 04

    Drug delivery & formulation

    Lipid nanoparticles (SLN, NLC), polymeric implants, hydrogel matrices and 3D-printed capsules engineered for controlled release of peptide therapeutics. Release-kinetics, encapsulation, PK and bioavailability studies.

  • 05

    Reproducibility, GMP & data integrity

    Industrial QC background under cGxP / GMP: HPLC analysis, audit-ready documentation and quantitative method validation — carried into every academic project since.
