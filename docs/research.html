Research interests

2015 — present

LC–MS Proteomics & Metaproteomics

High-throughput, high-sensitivity LC–MS/MS workflows on Orbitrap and timsTOF/Bruker platforms. Method development for label-free DDA and DIA acquisition across tissue, plasma, microbiome and food samples.

OrbitraptimsTOFDIA / DDA

Peptide Chemistry & Foldamers

Rational design and synthesis of host-defence peptides, α/β-peptides and peptidomimetic foldamers. Probing how chemically diverse AMPs converge on bacterial membrane hyperpolarization to potentiate antibiotics.

SPPSCD / NMRMIC / FACS

Drug Delivery & Formulation

Lipid nanoparticles (SLN, NLC), polymeric implants, hydrogel matrices and 3D-printed capsules engineered for controlled release of peptide therapeutics. Release-kinetics, encapsulation and PK studies.

Projects I've worked on

2015 — 2026
Nov 2025 – Apr 2026 University of Brighton INJECTHEAL · Horizon Europe

Postdoctoral Researcher — Self-administered injectable therapeutics

Worked within the EU Horizon-funded INJECTHEAL consortium on self-administered injectable formulations of biologics. Designed peptide-based nanocarriers for targeted delivery of therapeutics in wound healing. Analytical focus on peptide identity, integrity and stability, supporting consortium partners on formulation–stability decisions.

  • Developed and validated LC–MS/MS methods for peptide actives in complex formulation matrices
  • Stability and forced-degradation studies; degradation product identification
  • Performed SPPS and physicochemical characterisation including in vitro biocompatibility testing
  • Cross-partner data sharing and reporting under Horizon Europe deliverables
Feb – Jul 2025 University of East Anglia Norwich · Lecturer

Oral delivery of weight-loss peptide drugs

Designed and fabricated 3D-printed polymeric capsules to enhance the oral bioavailability of weight-loss peptide therapeutics. Combined polymer engineering with peptide formulation science and rigorous in vitro release-kinetics characterisation.

  • Polymer selection, capsule geometry optimisation and 3D-print parameter sweeps
  • Controlled-release profiles and stability under simulated GI conditions
  • Peptide release-kinetics analysis and quantitative data modelling
2018 – 2024 University of Szeged Martinek Lab · Doctoral

Bacterial membrane electrophysiology & antimicrobial peptides

Investigated how chemically diverse antimicrobial peptides converge on hyperpolarization of the bacterial membrane — the basis of my first-author Communications Biology paper. Synthesised AMPs and peptidomimetic foldamers via manual and automated SPPS, purified by HPLC–MS, characterised by CD, NMR and fluorescence.

  • Manual and automated SPPS; HPLC–MS purification and characterisation
  • CD, NMR, UV-Vis, fluorescence spectroscopy and ITC binding studies
  • Protein expression in E. coli; in vitro / in vivo assays (MIC, FACS)
  • Statistical analysis & figure preparation in R/RStudio
Oct 2017 – Feb 2018 Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Ltd. Halol, India · Industry

Pharmaceutical Quality Control — Analytical chemistry under GMP

HPLC analysis of pharmaceutical samples (finished APIs, intermediates, raw materials, recovered solvents, in-process material, working standards) in a fully GMP-compliant environment. Built audit-ready workflows with strict cGxP adherence and data integrity.

  • HPLC method execution and quantitative analysis on commercial products
  • Instrument maintenance, troubleshooting and calibration schedules
  • cGxP compliance, raw-data audit trails and analytical data integrity
Dec 2015 – Oct 2017 THSTI · Govt. of India Faridabad · JRF

Lipid & polymeric drug-delivery systems for tuberculosis

Formulated and characterised lipid nanoparticles, biodegradable polymeric & hydrogel implants and lipid-drug conjugates for sustained release of anti-TB therapeutics. Developed and validated HPLC methods for encapsulation efficiency and PK readouts in vitro and in vivo.

  • SLN / NLC formulation, hydrogel implants, lipid-drug conjugates
  • HPLC method development & validation; encapsulation and PK studies
  • Confocal/fluorescence biodistribution, cytokine assays; NMR, DLS, DSC-TGA
