Outreach & Teaching.

Science doesn't end at the lab bench. Alongside my research career I've taught at university level and worked directly with students in the community — committed to accessible, inclusive education and to the idea that good science communication matters.

Philosophy

Why I do this alongside research

Research is most valuable when it's communicated well and when the institutions that train future scientists are equitable and welcoming. Teaching gives me the chance to share the excitement of pharmaceutical science with the next generation. The exam invigilation work at City College Norwich keeps me grounded in the real, day-to-day experiences of students who deserve a fair chance — regardless of their learning needs or background.

Both roles reinforce each other: the attention to detail I bring to analytical chemistry translates directly into creating structured, fair assessment conditions; and the curiosity I try to spark in students feeds back into my own drive to keep learning.

"The smallest adjustments in how we assess and teach can make the biggest difference to an anxious student on exam day."

— Kaushik Nath Bhaumik

Exam Invigilator & Administrator
City College Norwich · Norwich, UK

Alongside my research and teaching work, I support exam delivery at City College Norwich — a further education college serving a diverse community of learners. The role connects my scientific precision with a genuine commitment to educational equity and student welfare.

Responsibilities

  • Invigilated paper-based and computer-based examinations, ensuring full compliance with regulations and maintaining exam security throughout
  • Prepared exam rooms, distributed materials, and provided clear candidate instructions to ensure an organised and calm assessment environment
  • Monitored candidates to prevent academic misconduct and addressed issues promptly and professionally
  • Acted as scribe and reader for students with special educational needs (SEN) and additional access requirements — transcribing responses and reading questions aloud to enable equal participation
  • Supported students with learning disabilities, physical disabilities and other access needs in a sensitive, professional and confidential manner
  • Contributed to administrative tasks: documentation, candidate registers and compliance records

"Supporting a student with special needs through an exam is one of the most direct ways I can contribute to educational equity. Every student deserves the conditions to do their best work."
