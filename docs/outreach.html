Teaching and Community

Alongside my research work, I'm passionate about the people-side of education. At City College Norwich I support exam delivery for a diverse student body — including learners with special educational needs and additional access requirements — ensuring fair, calm and inclusive conditions on the day.

The role gives me a clear view of the administrative challenges institutions face in delivering accessible assessment, and a deep appreciation for the small choices that make a big difference to anxious students. It keeps my academic work grounded in the lived realities of the people learning around me.

Responsibilities

  • Invigilated paper-based and computer-based examinations, ensuring full compliance with regulations and maintaining exam security throughout

  • Monitored candidates to prevent academic misconduct and addressed issues promptly and professionally
