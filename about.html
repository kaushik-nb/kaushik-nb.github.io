Bio

From Tripura to Norwich, by way of peptides.

I'm a pharmaceutical and bio-chemistry researcher with a multidisciplinary skill set spanning peptide chemistry, analytical chemistry / LC–MS, drug delivery and biological assays. My doctoral work at the University of Szeged in Hungary explored how antimicrobial peptides modulate bacterial electrophysiology — a sub-MIC strategy to enhance antibiotic efficacy — and gave me deep hands-on experience with peptide-level mass spectrometry.

Before the PhD, I worked in the Indian pharmaceutical industry at Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Ltd., performing GMP-compliant HPLC analysis, and at the Translational Health Science and Technology Institute (Government of India) developing lipid nanoparticle and polymeric implant drug delivery systems for tuberculosis treatment.

More recently I held a Lecturer position at the University of East Anglia in Norwich on oral delivery of weight-loss peptide drugs, and a Postdoctoral Fellowship at the University of Brighton on the EU Horizon-funded INJECTHEAL project — building LC–MS/MS workflows for peptide-based injectables. I'm now actively looking for analytical / proteomics roles where I can apply that LC–MS, DIA and data-analysis experience at scale.
